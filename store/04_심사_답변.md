## 반려 원인 분석 (11/18)

1. **'운세'는 지침 4.3(b)에 직접 이름이 나온 포화 분야다.** Apple 지침은 이미 앱이 넘치는 분야의 예로
   데이트·손전등·배경화면·타이머와 함께 fortune telling을 든다. 앱 이름 `하루 - 가계부·일정·날씨·운세`가
   흔한 기능 넷을 나열하고 그중 하나가 운세라서 4.3(b) 인상을 키운다. → 가장 유력한 원인
2. **방침·지원 페이지가 `claude.ai/artifact/...`(AI 서비스 도메인)에 있다.** 심사관에게 "AI 생성 서비스로 만든 앱"이라는
   신호로 읽혀 4.2.6이 붙었을 가능성이 있다. → 자체 주소로 옮긴다(`03_페이지_주소.md`의 "주소를 쓰는 곳" 전부 수정)
3. **설명 첫 문단이 "여러 기능을 한 앱에 모은"으로 시작한다.** 이 앱에만 있는 형식(달력 한 칸에 일정 + 그날 금액)이 뒤로 밀려 있다.

## 답장 본문 (영어 — 그대로 복사, 3,404자 · 입력 칸 제한 4,000자)

```
Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for reviewing Haru. My answers are below.

1. What the app does
Haru is a Korean daily planner built on one idea: schedule and spending belong on the same calendar. Each day cell in the month view shows that day's event titles and its income/expense totals together, so users do not need a separate calendar app and budget app.
- Budget periods can start on the user's payday (e.g., the 25th), matching how Korean salaried workers manage money. Spending analysis runs entirely on the device.
- The home screen shows today's events, to-dos, weekday routines, monthly totals and the local weather.
- Sign in with Apple or Google is optional; the app works fully in guest mode. Budget, schedule and to-do records are stored only on the device. No bank/card linking, ads or tracking SDKs.
- For weather, location is converted on the device to the Korea Meteorological Administration 5 km grid; only the grid number is sent to my server.

2. Intended users
Korean adults, mainly salaried workers and students in their 20s-40s, who plan on a calendar, budget around a monthly payday, and do not want to sign up or upload financial data.

3. Need in the market
Most Korean budget apps require sign-up and bank/card linking, and calendar apps do not show spending. Haru offers:
(a) one calendar showing events and daily income/expense together, with a one-tap day summary;
(b) payday-based budget periods and on-device spending analysis;
(c) an account-optional, local-first design;
(d) Korea-specific data: KMA forecasts, official public holidays, and lunar birthdates for the optional fortune, which requires in-app consent and has a report button for AI-generated content.

4. Beta testing
Since September 2026 I have run TestFlight with 8 external testers, plus Google Play closed testing. Changes made from tester feedback include:
- multi-day events shown as one continuous bar;
- start/end times and named colors (e.g., Work);
- a tap-a-date summary of events and spending;
- dated, important and recurring to-dos;
- monthly income/expense totals on Home;
- a choice of start screen;
- safe-area fixes for sheets and headers.

5. Standalone or suite
Standalone. It is the only app on my developer account.

6. Could it be part of another app on the account
No. There are no other apps on my account.

7. Shared code or assets with other apps on the account
No. There are no other apps on my account.

8. Shared codebase, SDK or content with third-party apps
No. The codebase is unique to Haru and uses only standard open-source frameworks (Expo / React Native, expo-sqlite, Zustand); the FastAPI backend is also specific to Haru. No template, white-label kit or no-code app builder was used. I used an AI coding assistant (Claude Code) as a development tool, like an IDE: I defined the features and design, and reviewed, tested and revised every change myself.

9. Created for a client or third party
No. Haru is my own concept, planned, designed, built and published by me as an individual developer.

Changes in this resubmission
I updated the app name, subtitle, description and screenshots to lead with the schedule-and-spending calendar; fortune and news are now described as secondary features. The privacy policy and support pages are now on my own server (haru-api.duckdns.org).

I am happy to provide a screen recording or more information if needed. Thank you.
```
