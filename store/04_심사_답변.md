## 반려 원인 분석 (11/18)

1. **'운세'는 지침 4.3(b)에 직접 이름이 나온 포화 분야다.** Apple 지침은 이미 앱이 넘치는 분야의 예로
   데이트·손전등·배경화면·타이머와 함께 fortune telling을 든다. 앱 이름 `하루 - 가계부·일정·날씨·운세`가
   흔한 기능 넷을 나열하고 그중 하나가 운세라서 4.3(b) 인상을 키운다. → 가장 유력한 원인
2. **방침·지원 페이지가 `claude.ai/artifact/...`(AI 서비스 도메인)에 있다.** 심사관에게 "AI 생성 서비스로 만든 앱"이라는
   신호로 읽혀 4.2.6이 붙었을 가능성이 있다. → 자체 주소로 옮긴다(`03_페이지_주소.md`의 "주소를 쓰는 곳" 전부 수정)
3. **설명 첫 문단이 "여러 기능을 한 앱에 모은"으로 시작한다.** 이 앱에만 있는 형식(달력 한 칸에 일정 + 그날 금액)이 뒤로 밀려 있다.

## 답장 본문 (영어 — 그대로 복사)

```
Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for reviewing Haru. I believe Haru meets guidelines 4.3(a), 4.3(b) and 4.2.6. Please find my answers to each question below.

1. What the app does and the primary problem it solves
Haru is a Korean-language daily planner built around one idea: your schedule and your spending belong on the same calendar. Each day cell in the month grid shows that day's event titles and that day's income/expense totals together, so users can see "what I did" and "what I spent" in one place instead of switching between a calendar app and a separate budget app.
- Budget periods can start on the user's payday (e.g., the 25th), which matches how Korean salaried workers actually manage money. Category spending by week/month/year is calculated entirely on the device.
- The home screen brings together today's events, dated to-dos, recurring weekday routines, the monthly income/expense totals, and the local weather forecast.
- Sign in with Apple or Google is optional. The app works fully without an account (guest mode), and budget, schedule and to-do records are stored only on the device (SQLite) either way. No bank or card linking, ads, or tracking/analytics SDKs are used.
- For weather, the device converts its approximate location into the Korea Meteorological Administration (KMA) 5 km forecast grid on-device, and only the grid number is sent to my server. Coordinates never leave the phone.

2. Intended users
Korean-speaking adults, mainly salaried workers and students in their 20s to 40s, who plan their days on a calendar and manage money around a monthly payday cycle, and who do not want to create an account or upload their financial data just to keep a simple budget.

3. Need or gap in the market
Most Korean budget apps require account sign-up and linking of bank or card accounts, while calendar apps do not show spending at all. Users who want a simple, private record of both end up using two apps and can never see them together. Haru addresses this with:
(a) a single calendar grid that shows events and daily income/expense totals together, plus a one-tap "at a glance" summary of a selected day;
(b) payday-based budget periods and on-device spending analysis;
(c) an account-optional, local-first design: personal records never leave the device;
(d) Korea-specific data: KMA grid forecasts with on-device location conversion, official Korean public holidays (Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute), and solar/lunar birthdates for the optional daily fortune. The AI fortune feature requires explicit in-app consent before any data is sent and includes a report button for AI-generated content.

4. Beta testing
Since September 2026 I have run TestFlight external testing with 8 testers across multiple builds, and I am also running Google Play closed testing. Feedback from TestFlight testers that I applied to the production build includes:
- Events that span several days can now be created with a start/end date and are shown as one continuous bar on the calendar.
- Events can have a start and end time, and each color can be given a name (e.g., Work, Exercise) to group events.
- Tapping a date opens an "at a glance" summary of that day's events and income/expenses.
- To-dos can be scheduled for a specific date, marked as important (pinned to the top), and set as weekday routines.
- The home screen now shows the month's total income and total expenses.
- Users can choose which screen the app opens on (Home, Calendar, Fortune or News).
- Input sheets and screen headers that overlapped the status bar were fixed to respect the device safe area.

5. Standalone product or part of a suite
Haru is a standalone product. It is the only app on my developer account.

6. Could the functionality be delivered within another app on the account
No. There are no other apps on my developer account, so there is no existing app it could be consolidated into.

7. Shared code, frameworks or assets with other apps on the account
No. Haru is the only app on my account, and its code and assets are not used in any other app.

8. Shared codebase, SDK or content library with third-party apps
No. Haru was written from scratch using only standard open-source frameworks (Expo / React Native, expo-sqlite, Zustand). The backend (FastAPI) that handles weather, news and holiday data parsing and caching is my own code. No commercial template, white-label kit, or app generation service was used.

9. Created for a client, partner or third party
No. Haru is my own concept and product, designed, developed and published by me as an individual developer. All content, branding and design are my own.

Thank you for your time. I am happy to provide additional screenshots, a screen recording, or any further information if needed.
```
